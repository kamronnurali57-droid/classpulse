CLASSPULSE — CLASS SURVEY DEMO

Open index.html in a browser to try it.
Files:
- index.html: page layout
- style.css: responsive styling
- script.js: 10 questions and survey flow

Features:
- Username entry
- 10 questions exactly
- Multiple-choice and typed-response questions
- Back/next controls and progress indicator
- Completion screen and answer review
- Works at each person's own pace in their browser

Important limitations:
This is a front-end mockup. Responses are not sent to a shared database and the survey owner cannot see classmates' answers. To collect answers from multiple classmates and view them together, you would need a backend/database or a survey service. Questions are phrased to avoid singling out or targeting classmates.
